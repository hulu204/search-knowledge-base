Today I had the pleasure of presenting my [recent work on the bag-of-documents model](https://maven.com/p/7270ba/a-bag-of-documents-model-for-query-understanding-retrieval) as part of a series of talks hosted by [Trey Grainger](https://www.treygrainger.com/) and [Doug Turnbull](https://softwaredoug.com/) in the context of their [AI-powered search classes](https://maven.com/search-school/ai-powered-search).

You can find the [slides](https://speakerdeck.com/dtunkelang/the-bag-of-documents-model-for-query-understanding-and-retrieval) below. I also encourage you to check out the [bag-of-documents Github repository](https://github.com/dtunkelang/bag-of-documents), the [dataset and model on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dtunkelang/bag-of-documents), as well as the live [Amazon demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/dtunkelang/bag-of-documents-demo) and [Best Buy demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/dtunkelang/bag-of-documents-bestbuy-demo). Enjoy!
