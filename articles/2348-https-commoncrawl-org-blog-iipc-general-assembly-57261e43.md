# IIPC General Assembly & Web Archiving Conference 2025

The Common Crawl team attended the 2025 IIPC General Assembly and Web Archiving Conference in Oslo, presenting recent work and participating in discussions on web preservation.

[![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-002](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-002.webp)

Thom Vaughan

Thom is Principal Engineer at the Common Crawl Foundation.](https://commoncrawl.org/team/thom-vaughan)

![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-003](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-003.webp)

Last week members of the Common Crawl Foundation team (Sebastian Nagel, Pedro Ortiz Suarez, and Thom Vaughan) attended the 2025 [IIPC](https://netpreserve.org/) General Assembly (GA) and Web Archiving Conference (WAC), hosted by the [National Library of Norway](https://www.nb.no/en/) in Oslo.  As [new members of the IIPC](https://netpreserve.org/about-us/members/common-crawl-foundation/), we are thrilled to join a global community of organizations committed to preserving the web for future generations, and to have the chance to present some of our work among colleagues in the web archiving space.

![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-004](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-004.webp)

*National Library of Norway: the home of Norwegian knowledge and heritage in Oslo*

Common Crawl delivered a range of contributions including poster presentations, lightning talks, and a workshop. These were very well received, and we appreciated the many conversations that followed.

![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-005](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-005.webp)

*Sebastian Nagel presenting for Common Crawl at the IIPC General Assembly 2025 at the National Library of Norway*

We had the opportunity to (re)connect with representatives from several national libraries, including those of Norway, Sweden, Denmark, France, and the Netherlands, as well as researchers and professionals from industry and academia.

![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-006](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-006.webp)

*Pedro Ortiz Suarez presenting at the IIPC WAC 2025 for Common Crawl on ARC and WARC formats*

Among our lightning talks, posters, and workshops, our team gave presentations during the [General Assembly](https://netpreserve.org/ga2025/abstracts/) and [Web Archiving Conference](https://netpreserve.org/ga2025/programme/wac/) on:

* the [rocky road to converting ARC to WARC formats](https://github.com/commoncrawl/arc2warc-conversion),
* ‍[Asynchronous and Modular Pipelines for Fast WARC Annotation](https://netpreserve.org/resources/WAC25_POSTER15_ORTIZ-SUAREZ-VAUGHAN.pdf),
* ‍[Politely Downloading Millions of WARC Files Without Burning the Servers Down](https://netpreserve.org/resources/WAC25_POSTER16_ORTIZ-SUAREZ-VAUGHAN-LINDHAL.pdf) via [cc-downloader](https://github.com/commoncrawl/cc-downloader),
* ‍[Crawler Politeness in the Age of GenAI](https://netpreserve.org/resources/WAC25_POSTER17_NAGEL-VAUGHAN.pdf),
* ‍[Crawling with HTTP/2](https://netpreserve.org/resources/WAC25_POSTER18_NAGEL.pdf) and
* a [hands-on workshop](https://github.com/commoncrawl/wac2025-webgraph-workshop) on using Common Crawl’s [Web Graph](https://commoncrawl.org/web-graphs) releases.

![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-007](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-007.webp)

*Thom Vaughan presenting at the IIPC WAC 2025 for Common Crawl on the Robots Exclusion Protocol*

Our team also met with Stephan Oepen from the University of Oslo, and colleagues from the [End of Term Archive](https://eotarchive.org/) project with whom we’ve collaborated on the EOT 2024: Ilya Kreymer of [Webrecorder](https://webrecorder.net/), Sawood Alam of the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/), and Mark Phillips of the [University of North Texas Libraries](https://library.unt.edu/).

![2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43-fig-008](../images/2348-https-commoncrawl-org-blog-iipc-general-assembly-57261e43/figure-008.webp)

*Left to right: Thom Vaughan, Ilya Kreymer, Sawood Alam, Mark Phillips, Pedro Ortiz Suarez, Sebastian Nagel: members of the End of Term Archive team met at the IIPC WAC 2025*

We’re looking forward to more discussions with our friends (new and old) from the IIPC in the near future.

## Erratum:

## Content is truncated

Originally reported by:

[More details](/errata/content-is-truncated)

Some archived content is truncated due to fetch size limits imposed during crawling. This is necessary to handle infinite or exceptionally large data streams (e.g., radio streams). Prior to March 2025 (CC-MAIN-2025-13), the truncation threshold was 1 MiB. From the March 2025 crawl onwards, this limit has been increased to 5 MiB.
