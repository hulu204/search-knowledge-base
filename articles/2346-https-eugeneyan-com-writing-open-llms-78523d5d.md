# Open-LLMs - A list of LLMs for Commercial Use

[
[llm](/tag/llm/)
[misc](/tag/misc/)
]
 · 1 min read

I was looking for a list of open LLMs with commercial licenses and casually asked on a discord channel. (The goal was to finetune an LLM—that had a commercial license—to address legal and privacy concerns around using third-party LLMs.)

![Image](/assets/open-llm-qn.webp "Image")

Exploring open LLMs on a lazy weekend.

After googling around for a bit, I realize there wasn’t a list compiled (yet). Thus, I decided to answer my own question and help others on this same path. You can find that list [here](https://github.com/eugeneyan/open-llms).

![Image](/assets/open-llms.webp "Image")

A list of open LLMs, including those finetuned for code.

I’m surprised by the support from the community. In two days, we’ve seen [eight PRs](https://github.com/eugeneyan/open-llms/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Aclosed) that added more open LLMs. This includes a [through discussion on adding model details](https://github.com/eugeneyan/open-llms/issues/7) followed by a [PR](https://github.com/eugeneyan/open-llms/pull/11) that added context length as a column. There was also a [correction on an LLM](https://twitter.com/sasikiran_m/status/1654889449334128642) that didn’t actually have commercial license. I’m grateful for the support so far and am looking forward to working with the community to add datasets, evals, and more.

Have a suggestion or contribution? Raise an [issue](https://github.com/eugeneyan/open-llms/issues) or [pull request](https://github.com/eugeneyan/open-llms/pulls). Thanks in advance!
